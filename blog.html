Guide · 6 min

Launch a brokerage in 2026

Order matters: licence, platform, liquidity, CRM, payments. Wrong order means rebuilding what you already bought.

Choose your model first. A-book, B-book, or hybrid? Retail FX or a niche? The model decides licence, liquidity, risk.

Buy the stack, not the parts. One integrated setup launches in 2–4 weeks. Talk to us for a launch plan.

Platforms · 5 min

MT5 vs cTrader: pick yours

MT5-type is the default: traders know it, tools are everywhere, all assets in one terminal.

cTrader feels cleaner and faster, with depth of market. Strong for active, tech-comfortable traders.

Many brokers run both. Start with your clients' platform, add the other later. See platforms.

Good brokerage support, defined

Traders remember withdrawals and 2am answers. Support is retention, not a cost centre.

First response in minutes, with engineers behind the front line — not guesses.

Monitoring prevents most tickets. The best ticket never opens. Ours runs 24/7 — more about us.

Liquidity · 5 min

Liquidity explained simply

One question: when a client clicks buy, how good is the price — and how much size?

Aggregation beats one source. Several providers mean sharper prices and deeper books.

Routing matters too. Smart routing finds the best fill; bad routing shows as slippage. See liquidity.

CRM · 4 min

Your CRM is your growth engine

Onboarding, KYC, IBs, deposits, retention — that's where growth is won or lost.

Onboarding is the first product. If clients can't verify and deposit smoothly, you lose them early.

IBs compound: partners bring traders, traders bring volume. See our Forex CRM — from $500.

Prop trading · 5 min

Prop challenges that actually work

Prop firms live on trust: fair rules, accurate dashboards, payouts that arrive.

Keep rules simple and visible — target, drawdown, daily loss. If traders need a lawyer, your rules failed.

Build payouts first: request, approval, payment — before selling challenge one. See prop trading.
