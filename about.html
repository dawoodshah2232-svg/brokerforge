About BrokerForge

A Dubai-based technology team for brokers, prop firms and fintechs — building the stack, so you can build the business.

Who we are

Engineers and operators, not salespeople.

BrokerForge started with a simple observation: launching a brokerage took too long, cost too much, and stitched together too many vendors who blamed each other when something broke. So we built one team that owns the whole stack — platform, liquidity, CRM, risk and payments — and launches it in weeks.

Today we serve clients in 53 countries, with 35+ solutions across the brokerage lifecycle, and a support team that answers around the clock.

  • Dubai headquarters — client onboarding and partnerships
  • Istanbul engineering — integrations and platform work
  • Remote support — 24/7, across time zones
