Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026.

What we collect

When you contact BrokerForge — through the form on this site, by phone, or on WhatsApp — we collect the details you give us: your name, email address, phone number, company and the content of your message. We also receive basic technical information when you visit this site, such as your browser type and the pages you visit.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote, and provide the services you ask for. We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising to third parties.

How we store it

Enquiry details are kept only as long as needed to respond and, where a business relationship follows, for the duration of that relationship and any legal retention period. Access is limited to the BrokerForge team members who need it to do their work.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers. If we add basic, privacy-respecting analytics in future, this policy will be updated first.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. To do so, contact us on +971 52 322 2946 or via WhatsApp.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
