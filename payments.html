KYC & AML

Compliance built into the flow, not bolted on.

Clients upload documents during onboarding, your team reviews them in the CRM, and verified clients can deposit right away. Screening and audit trails keep your compliance team covered.

  • Document upload and verification workflows
  • Identity checks via your chosen KYC providers
  • AML screening and full audit trails
  • Swap-free / Islamic accounts supported on request

Note: swap-free account availability depends on your platform and liquidity setup — confirm it in your quote.
